C Said and shown

Makes the jury's two claims visible. "It knows me": an account question shows its numbers on a slip above the consent card's dashed line, labelled that only the answer crosses it. "Confidence gap with evidence": the summary puts what you said next to what we saw, then the level, then three numbered focus areas. Trade-off: the most explicit privacy and gap story, but the slip repeats the numbers already in the question and the split card is tight at 390 px with long because lines.

Reduced motion: the progress line and summary blocks change without movement. Without it: the progress line grows on Next (200 ms ease-out, transform: scaleX), and summary blocks rise 8 px with a 50 ms stagger. Buttons scale to 0.97 on press.
