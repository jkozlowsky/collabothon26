# Entire story of Alex
Alex is 29 and lives in Frankfurt. He earns €2,150 net a month and has about €880 left over each month. He has €4,280 in his current account and €12,500 in his savings account. He and his partner want to buy a flat in 2–3 years, but his savings have no clear purpose. For the past year and a half he has put €100 a month into an ETF, without much knowledge or confidence.

Alex uses the Commerzbank app every day. He opens the Learn tab and agrees that his learning can be tailored to his bank data. From that data we can see that Alex saves regularly, has a safety net and invests small amounts. We only ask about what the data cannot tell us: his stage of life, what he wants to learn more about (his goal) and how he manages his money today. Alex says he is saving for a flat in 2–3 years, gets the question on real interest rates wrong and rates his confidence at 2 out of 5.

The app suggests the "Saving" area, starting from the basics. Alex completes a lesson on inflation: a short explanation, an interactive example, a quiz and a decision scenario. Within two weeks he finishes the whole area and tests his knowledge in duels in the Quiz House.

The summary shows what he has learned and reminds him of his goal. Alex makes the decision himself: he keeps €7,500 as a six-month safety net and puts €5,000 into a 24-month fixed-term deposit at a fixed rate. The deposit appears in his bank account straight away. Alex gets an informed decision and money that matches his goal. The bank gets a two-year deposit and an engaged customer. The next area is "Long-term investing".
