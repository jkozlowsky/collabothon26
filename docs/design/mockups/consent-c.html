C The line

The consent card draws the bank's boundary: what stays inside (greyed, unreadable transactions) above a dashed line, and the slip of summaries that crosses it below. The promise is the label on the line itself. Loading draws the learning path station by station, with the first lessons waiting, still locked, at the end.

Reduced motion: the ring around the active station holds still at half strength, the petrol line jumps to the next station, and stations change state without a transition. Without it: the ring pings every 1.4 s and the line grows down to the next station (320 ms ease-in-out).
