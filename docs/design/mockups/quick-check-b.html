B The route

Carries the loading screen's stations through the whole step. The four questions are stations on a dashed line that turns petrol as you answer (petrol, not green: an answered question is not a correct one). The summary is the same line drawn downwards: what we saw (the gap), who you are, where to start, ending at a yellow station for the first lesson. Trade-off: the strongest story from consent to lesson, but the route needs its own component and gets long with three focus areas.

Reduced motion: the petrol line jumps to the next station and summary stations appear in place. Without it: the line grows to the next station on Next (240 ms ease-in-out), and summary stations rise with a 50 ms stagger. Buttons scale to 0.97 on press.
