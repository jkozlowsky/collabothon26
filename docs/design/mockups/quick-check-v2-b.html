B The map after every answer

Quick check v2: no typing. An adaptive test with 3 to 6 single-choice questions, one confidence step per answer, and a live map of five areas. Questions stay clean: just a segmented progress bar. After each answer, the confidence chips appear in the thumb zone at the foot, and a full-screen route interstitial (1.7 s, tap Continue to skip) shows all five stations. The one you just answered fills and is marked Updated, then the next station gets a yellow ring and "Up next": that is the thinking state. Trade-off: the biggest, most readable map and the strongest "it is working on you" moment, but it interrupts the flow four times.

Reduced motion: Without reduced motion: bars fill over 900 ms and the next station pings. With it: bars jump and the ring holds still; labels still change, so nothing is lost.
