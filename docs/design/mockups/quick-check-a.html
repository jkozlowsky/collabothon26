A Segments

The quiet version of "The line": one question per page on the canvas, a four-segment bar under the back button, and account numbers marked inline with one muted line saying they stay on the phone. The summary stacks familiar cards: a petrol gap card, the learner level with its because lines, then the focus areas as list rows. Trade-off: safest to build (reuses the onboarding page and BankCard-style rows), but the least memorable on stage.

Reduced motion: segments fill without a transition and summary cards appear in place. Without it: the current segment fills over 200 ms ease-out on Next, and summary cards rise 8 px with a 50 ms stagger (260 ms). Buttons scale to 0.97 on press. No right/wrong feedback between questions.
